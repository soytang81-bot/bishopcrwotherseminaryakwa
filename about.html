OUR APPROACH

potential to grow.

Bishop Crowther Seminary, Awka, is committed to nurturing learners through purposeful education and positive values.

We aim to create an environment where learners are supported to develop knowledge, confidence, self-discipline and care for others. We believe a strong education combines academic growth with the habits and values needed for life.
