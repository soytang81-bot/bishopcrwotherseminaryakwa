WHO WE ARE

An education that
opens possibilities.

Bishop Crowther Seminary, Awka, is committed to helping learners develop academically, socially and personally.

We value curiosity, discipline, compassion and the confidence to make a positive contribution. Our goal is to work with families to create a school experience where every learner is seen, encouraged and challenged to do their best.
